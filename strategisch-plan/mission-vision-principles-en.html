Mission, vision and guiding principles

Mission

The Faculty of Economics and Business Administration is a collegiate student-centric Faculty, that dares to think today about the challenges of tomorrow, welcoming all students to participate in high quality, research-based study programs and conducting rigorous multi-perspective scientific research, starting from relevant real-life business problems and societal challenges.

Vision

Be recognized for our high quality and innovative research and education.

Be the critical eye opener for business practice and government.

Our guiding principles

Thrive

An environment in which students, faculty, staff and alumni reach their full potential. Excellent research and high-quality education go hand in hand with a culture of continuous improvement, creativity, integrity and entrepreneurship, aimed at a positive impact on society, locally and globally.

Dare to think

Intellectual courage, curiosity and innovation. We question conventional wisdom, value diverse perspectives and encourage collaboration across disciplines. In this way we shape lifelong learners and leaders who dare to take on complex challenges.

Inter-Act

Collaboration between students, faculty, staff, alumni and external partners. Through interaction across networks, disciplines and perspectives we build a lively ecosystem that reaches beyond academia and contributes to sustainable solutions for real-world questions.
